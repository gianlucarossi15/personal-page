Gianluca Rossi received the Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Computer Engineering from the University of Padova, Italy.
He is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree at Lyon 1 University, France, with the CNRS LIRIS laboratory, working on graphs and time-series data.

## Research

I work at the intersection of graph algorithms, time series analysis, and database systems, with a particular interest in how graph and time series data can be modeled and queried together.

- **Graph & Time Series Integration** — designing algorithms that unify graph traversals with time series joins over graphs carrying time series data.
- **Analytics** — developing query processing and analytics techniques for extracting insights from these combined graph and time series workloads.
- **Graph & Time Series Workloads** — building and evaluating data management systems that support these workloads at scale.
