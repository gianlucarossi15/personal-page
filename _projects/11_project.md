**Comune di Thiene** — Thiene, Italy · 15/06/2018 – 07/2018

During my internship, I developed a web application that allows the employees of Comune di Thiene to search for the work phone numbers of other colleagues.

I built the client-side part using raw HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

For the server-side, I used PHP to handle user login and to manage the database, which held records composed of employee name, phone number, and office.
