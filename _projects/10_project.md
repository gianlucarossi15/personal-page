**Mètis Vela Unipd** — Padova, Italy · 16/10/2021 – 06/2024

I work on the sailing boat team of the University of Padova, in the electronics and IT sector.

My tasks include developing the Real-Time Operating System, written in C++, that controls the boat's microcontroller and attached sensors.

I designed and built a sensor for measuring shroud forces on the mast, giving the crew a live readout of the forces applied to the shrouds.
